# ASCII Sigil Language - Gallery of Magical Constructs
# Each line is a valid sigil with semantic meaning!

# === BASIC ELEMENTS ===
# Single element: Raw fire
asterisk

# Single element: Pure water
tilde

# === SIMPLE CONTAINERS ===
# Amplified fire: <*> - Increases energy of fire
langle asterisk rangle

# Stabilized water: [~] - Grounds the flowing water
lbrack tilde rbrack

# Channeled air: (^) - Directs the swift air
lparen caret rparen

# === ELEMENT FUSION (Transformers) ===
# Steam: {*+~} - Fire and water create steam!
lbrace asterisk plus tilde rbrace

# Lightning: {*+^} - Fire and air create lightning!
lbrace asterisk plus caret rbrace

# Mud: {#+~} - Earth and water create mud!
lbrace hash plus tilde rbrace

# === POWER AMPLIFICATION ===
# Double fire: ** - Intense flames
asterisk asterisk

# Triple fire in amplifier: <***> - EXTREME heat!
langle asterisk asterisk asterisk rangle

# === NESTED CONTAINERS ===
# Stabilized amplified fire: [<*>] - Volatile energy, safely contained
lbrack langle asterisk rangle rbrack

# Amplified steam: <{*+~}> - Superheated steam!
langle lbrace asterisk plus tilde rbrace rangle

# Channeled lightning: ({*+^}) - Controlled electrical energy
lparen lbrace asterisk plus caret rbrace rparen

# === COMPLEX COMPOSITIONS ===
# Fire and water side by side: * ~ - Opposing forces
asterisk tilde

# Amplified fire beside earth: <*> # - Heat meets solid ground
langle asterisk rangle hash

# Triple element fusion: {*+~+^} - Fire, water, and air combine!
lbrace asterisk plus tilde plus caret rbrace

# === DEEPLY NESTED SIGILS ===
# Stabilized channeled steam: [({*+~})] - Triple containment!
lbrack lparen lbrace asterisk plus tilde rbrace rparen rbrack

# Amplified stabilized fire: <[*]> - Grounded energy, then amplified
langle lbrack asterisk rbrack rangle

# Void channeling lightning: @ ({*+^}) - Mystery directs power
at lparen lbrace asterisk plus caret rbrace rparen

# === POWER STRUCTURES ===
# Multiple amplified fires: <*> <*> <*> - Array of heat sources
langle asterisk rangle langle asterisk rangle langle asterisk rangle

# Fused double elements: {**+~~} - Intense fire meets intense water
lbrace asterisk asterisk plus tilde tilde rbrace

# === THE ULTIMATE SIGIL ===
# Nested fusion in amplifier with void: <{@+{*+~}}> - Void absorbs steam, then amplified!
